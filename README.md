## Hi, I'm Ashley!

Skills: C++, C, Python, Java, R, VS Code, IntelliJ, Unit testing, Problem Solving, Leadership
Academics: Programming Practicum, Data Structures, Machine Organization, Systems Programming

Ever since I was a kid, I’ve been fascinated by how things work. In high school, that curiosity led me to want to learn more about technology. I took my first programming class in my junior year, and it inspired me to take all the other CS classes my high school offered. I made some of my first projects and games in those classes, and they really helped create a love for programming. Those classes made the decision to major in computer science easy, leading me where I am today.

Since then, I’ve continued to grow through projects, coursework, and hands-on experience. I decided to participate in the 2023 Fitch Group Codeathon. I worked with three other students and two mentors to revamp The Big Shoulder’s Fund website. I am proud to say that my group won the codeathon because the organization liked our ideas for making the website more accessible to people and easier to navigate.

This inspired me to apply for my first internship as a Computer Science Education Intern at the end of my first year. My partner and I worked under one of our Professors to help create projects for the students, and I assisted in managing and organizing the data to be sent to other professors for Program Design I. As I neared the end of the internship, we began researching the preconceptions students held about Discrete Mathematics and how it affects their progress and understanding in the class. My partner and I were offered the opportunity to transition into a research assistant position for the following year. 

During this time as an Undergraduate Research Assistant, I helped create and distribute surveys to gauge students’ preconceptions and prior knowledge. Then I analyzed the data for any trends or patterns. Throughout this process, my partner and I began drafting our research paper to present our findings. We concluded the year by submitting the paper and giving our code to our professor for future research analysis. 

The skills I developed in communication, collaboration, and data analysis have greatly improved my learning both in and out of the classroom throughout this time. Part of my love for CS stems from the fact that technology continues to grow and change. I love to learn, and being in a field that experiences continuous growth is exactly what I strive for. I’m excited to see how much more I can accomplish in the future.
